Neolife Wellness Center provides Ayurvedic consultation booking, doctor appointment booking, online and offline wellness consultation, video consultation, Ayurvedic therapy booking, sale of Ayurvedic medicines, herbal supplements, wellness products, wellness education, online payments, lifestyle guidance, and related wellness services.

By accessing or using our platform, users consent to the collection, processing, storage, disclosure, and protection of their information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.
