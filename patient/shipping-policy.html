At Neolife Wellness Center, we strive to deliver your orders safely, securely, and on time. Please read our Shipping Policy carefully before placing an order.

Order Processing

  • Orders are processed within 1–2 business days.
  • Orders placed on Sundays or public holidays will be processed on the next working day.

Shipping Charges

  • Free Shipping across Karnataka.
  • Standard shipping charges apply for deliveries to other states in India.
  • Shipping charges are calculated and displayed during checkout.

Estimated Delivery Time

Location Estimated Delivery
Karnataka 3–5 Business Days

Delivery timelines are estimates and may vary due to courier delays, weather conditions, natural disasters, public holidays, or other unforeseen circumstances.

Order Tracking

Once your order has been shipped, you will receive a tracking number via SMS and/or email. You can use this tracking number to monitor your shipment's progress.

Customers are responsible for providing accurate shipping information. Neolife Wellness Center is not responsible for delivery delays or failed deliveries caused by incorrect or incomplete addresses.
